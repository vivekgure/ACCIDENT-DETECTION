System Architecture

Lightweight local inference paired with multi-frame kinematic validation.

01

Lightweight Object Detection

YOLOv8n runs locally on CUDA or CPU at 640px (with 480px and 384px performance modes) to classify cars, motorcycles, buses, trucks, bicycles, and pedestrians.

02

ByteTrack Motion Kinematics

Assigns persistent tracking IDs across frames to compute smoothed velocity, closing vectors, deceleration shockwaves, and trajectory deflection angles.

03

4-Tier Temporal Validation

Separates vehicle overlap from genuine collisions by requiring sustained multi-frame kinematic evidence before confirming an accident and saving snapshots.
